# Contributions

Changes to the covered SameScale project source are accepted under Apache-2.0,
as described in [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [LICENSE_SCOPE.md](LICENSE_SCOPE.md).
You retain your copyright; contributions do not transfer ownership to a new
company. Before submitting, ensure you have the right to license your contribution,
including any applicable employer authorization. Apache-2.0 section 5 governs
intentional contributions unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Identify copied/adapted third-party material, its exact source/version, license
and copyright/attribution notices. Preserve those notices and update the third-party
inventory when dependencies or bundled material change. Do not present external
code, fonts, icons or images as original SameScale work. A license field alone is
not a complete binary redistribution audit.

[Brand artwork](BRAND_ASSETS.md), external/archived evidence and other excluded
material require a separate rights decision; do not infer an Apache grant for
them from a source-code contribution. This document creates no retroactive CLA
or blanket consent by past contributors.

Follow [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and the existing verification instructions in
[README](README.md). Preserve immutable evidence, identify synthetic results,
and keep credentials and private reasoning out of submissions. A source
contribution or open-source license does not authorize deployments, real Agent/
Provider execution or OpenAI SIWC access.
